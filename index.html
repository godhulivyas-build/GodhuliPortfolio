    I didn’t start with an app.
    I started in the field.

    01The itch

    Agriculture is my background, and one thing kept bothering me: a farmer’s crop reaches the buyer late.

    02The legwork

    Before any design, I went to the mandis, villages and fields and talked to farmers, traders and transporters.

    300+ conversations

    03The finding

    The delays traced back to one thing: everyone coordinating by phone call.

    70% of delays

    04The call

    So I cut the scope to dispatch first and made it voice-first, in the farmer’s own language.

    318+ farmers registered
    1. 1 · Speak in your language
    2. 2 · Dispatch gets matched
    3. 3 · Crop reaches the buyer
