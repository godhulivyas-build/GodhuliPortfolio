01 · Product Overview

Two workflows, one ledger.

Peakflo automates both sides of the cash cycle: money going out and money coming in, wired into the ERP a finance team already trusts.

Core Product

Procure-to-Pay

Automates purchase requests, multi-level approvals, PO creation, three-way invoice matching, and payment execution in a single workflow.

Core Product

Invoice-to-Cash

Manages invoice generation, delivery, collections follow-up, payment reconciliation, and cash posting, reducing Days Sales Outstanding.

Infrastructure

ERP Integrations

Bidirectional sync with NetSuite, Xero, QuickBooks, and SAP, connecting invoice status, payment data, and general ledger entries.

Market context. Mid-market finance teams ($5M–$500M revenue) in Southeast Asia and India still process invoices via email chains, spreadsheets, and manual approval routing, creating reconciliation backlogs, late payment penalties, and zero real-time cash visibility. Peakflo's core thesis is eliminating these operational gaps with AI-native automation.

04 · Friction Map

Three problems, traced to root cause.

Not three symptoms — three specific breakdowns, each with a mechanism and a rupee number attached.

Critical Impact

No Automated Approval Escalation

Invoices stall in multi-level approval hierarchies when approvers are unavailable, on leave, travelling, or overloaded. There is no trigger that fires when an SLA window is breached. AP managers manually track outstanding approvals and send ad-hoc reminders via email, with no visibility into where an invoice is blocked or why.

Root causeApproval routing logic is static. The system assigns approvers but does not enforce a time-bound escalation path. No event fires on SLA breach.
Business impactAverage 3–5 day delay per invoice. For a company processing 200 invoices/month, this compounds to 600–1,000 delayed processing days monthly, directly increasing Days Payable Outstanding and straining vendor relationships.
High Impact

Duplicate Invoice Detection Gap

Vendors re-submit invoices due to network timeouts, lack of confirmation, or accounting errors. Without AI-based similarity detection across invoice fields, AP teams process duplicates or catch them during month-end reconciliation, weeks after the payment has been made and the error has cascaded into financial reporting.

Root causeInvoice ingestion relies on manual PO number lookup rather than multi-field similarity scoring across vendor ID, amount, date range, and line item hash.
Business impactIndustry average duplicate payment rate is 0.1–0.5% of invoice volume. At $10M monthly payables, this represents $10,000–$50,000 in overpayments per month before reconciliation correction, plus the operational cost of recovery and vendor dispute resolution.
Medium Impact

Batch ERP Sync Creates Stale Decision Data

Invoice status, payment confirmations, and approval outcomes sync to ERP (NetSuite, Xero, QuickBooks) on a batch schedule, hourly or end-of-day. Finance teams and CFOs operate on data that is 1–8 hours old during the sync window. Early payment discount windows (typically 48 hours) are consistently missed when the payables position is not visible in real time.

Root causeThe sync layer is batch-based rather than event-driven. No webhook fires when an invoice changes status; instead, changes accumulate for the next scheduled sync cycle.
Business impactEarly payment discounts of 2–3% on eligible invoices are routinely missed. At $2M monthly eligible payables, that is $40,000–$60,000 in uncaptured savings annually. CFO dashboards show a trailing position, not current cash exposure.

05 · Proposed Solutions

TO-BE: three agentic fixes.

Each fix targets one root cause directly, not the symptom around it.

Solution 01 · P0 · Highest Priority

Agentic AI Auto-Escalation Trigger

60% ↓ approval cycle time

Build an event-driven agentic workflow that monitors approval SLA compliance in real time. When a defined threshold is breached, the system automatically escalates to the next approver tier, generates a context-aware notification, and logs the action for audit, eliminating the manual tracking burden from AP teams entirely.

  • Invoice status = "pending approval" + elapsed time exceeds configurable SLA threshold (default: 24h).
  • Agentic trigger fires: LLM generates escalation message with invoice context, urgency level, and approver history.
  • Approval re-routed to next tier via REST API; notification sent via email, Slack, and WhatsApp simultaneously.
  • Escalation logged with timestamp, reason code, and prior approver chain for compliance audit trail.
Technical approachEvent trigger on SLA breach → LLM-generated notification (context-aware) → Approval routing API → Multi-channel webhook (email/Slack/WhatsApp) → Audit log write

Solution 02 · P1 · High Priority

AI-Powered Duplicate Invoice Detection

94% ↓ duplicate payment rate

On every invoice ingestion event, run a multi-field similarity check against the full invoice history using embedding-based matching. Invoices above an 85% similarity confidence score are routed to an exception queue for human review before entering the payment pipeline, preventing overpayments at the point of entry rather than catching them at month-end.

  • Extract invoice fingerprint: vendor ID + amount + date range (±7 days) + line item hash.
  • Lightweight embedding model computes cosine similarity against open and closed invoice history.
  • Score ≥ 85%: routed to exception queue with match explanation; score below threshold: normal processing continues.
Technical approachInvoice ingestion webhook → embedding model (metadata-only, not full-document LLM) → cosine similarity scoring → confidence threshold routing → exception queue UI with match reason

Solution 03 · P2 · Medium Priority

Event-Driven ERP Sync

8hr → <2min lag

Replace the batch sync layer with a webhook-triggered, event-driven sync architecture. Every invoice status change (approved, paid, rejected, disputed) fires an immediate API call to the connected ERP, with an idempotent retry queue ensuring no event is dropped on API failure. This makes the CFO cash flow dashboard current to the minute, not the hour.

  • Invoice status change event fires in Peakflo (any state transition).
  • Webhook dispatched immediately to ERP API endpoint (NetSuite / Xero / QuickBooks).
  • Sync confirmed; idempotent retry queue handles API failures with exponential backoff.
Technical approachState change event → outbound webhook → ERP REST API (per integration) → idempotent retry queue → sync status audit log → CFO dashboard real-time feed

07 · Implementation Roadmap

Four weeks to a staged pilot.

Weeks 1–2

Phase 1 · Discovery

Structured discovery sessions with AP managers across customer segments. Map the full invoice lifecycle end to end in at least 3 accounts, recording every manual step, handoff, and decision point. Establish metric baselines: current average approval cycle time, duplicate invoice rate, and ERP sync lag by customer tier. Non-negotiable before any build decision.

Week 3

Phase 2 · PRD

Specify escalation trigger conditions, configurable SLA thresholds per approval tier, notification channel logic (email / Slack / WhatsApp), audit trail data model, and rollback mechanism for incorrectly escalated invoices. Detailed enough that engineering can scope without a meeting, acceptance criteria written before a single line of code.

Week 4

Phase 3 · Sprint Kickoff

Decompose the PRD into sprint-ready epics and user stories with explicit acceptance criteria. Instrument metric baselines in analytics. Identify 2 pilot customers for controlled beta. Target: first internal demo of the escalation trigger in staging by end of week 4, with failure modes tested, not just the happy path.

Phase 3 outcome: PRD signed off · Sprint 1 started · Baselines instrumented · Auto-escalation in staging · 2 pilot customers confirmed

08 · Open Questions

What I'd want to know next.

A teardown from the outside has limits. These are the five things I'd verify before writing a line of the real spec.

  1. 01What is the actual average approval cycle time across customer accounts today, and is SLA breach data tracked per approval tier or only at the invoice level?
  2. 02What percentage of customers have 3+ level approval hierarchies, and are tier configurations managed per customer, per invoice amount threshold, or both?
  3. 03Is the ERP sync layer fully batch-based, or have any status events (e.g. payment confirmations) already been partially migrated to webhooks?
  4. 04Has duplicate invoice detection appeared as a recurring support category, and is there data on the frequency or dollar-value of overpayment incidents reported by customers?
  5. 05How is the agentic AI workflow infrastructure architected: are prompts and routing logic managed centrally, or configured per customer workflow instance?
