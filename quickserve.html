Product Requirements Document · Two-Sided Marketplace

QuickServe — Rapido,
for salon and beauty services.

Salon and beauty services in India are largely unstructured and offline: customers depend on phone calls and personal references, freelance beauticians face inconsistent demand and visibility. QuickServe applies the same discovery-and-fulfilment structure Rapido used for bike taxis to on-demand, at-home salon services.

01 · Background & Problem

Two sides, two very different frustrations.

With rising preference for at-home services, users expect predictable pricing, flexible scheduling, trusted professionals, and a digital-first booking experience — none of which the offline market gives them.

QuickServe, the pitch

User problems

  • Difficulty finding reliable beauticians at short notice
  • No real-time availability visibility
  • Price ambiguity and lack of transparency
  • Poor experience with cancellations and delays

Provider problems

  • Irregular demand and income
  • No centralised booking system
  • Limited digital presence
  • Inefficient schedule management

05 · Core Features

Five must-haves, two should-haves.

Service Discovery

Must have
  • Category-based browsing
  • Price and duration visibility
  • Ratings and reviews

Why: Reduces decision friction and increases booking confidence.

Real-Time Availability & Scheduling

Must have
  • Live time slots
  • Provider availability sync
  • Reschedule and cancel rules

Why: Prevents double-booking and drop-offs.

Booking & Fulfilment

Must have
  • Instant booking confirmation
  • Provider assignment
  • Status tracking: accepted, on the way, completed

Why: Core marketplace fulfilment loop.

Payments

Must have
  • UPI and card payments
  • Transparent pricing breakup
  • Promo codes

Why: Reduces cash dependency and improves trust.

Ratings & Reviews

Must have
  • Post-service rating
  • Written feedback

Why: Quality control and trust flywheel.

Provider Dashboard

Should have
  • Earnings tracking
  • Booking history
  • Availability management

Why: Improves provider retention.

In-App Communication

Should have
  • Chat and call between user and provider
  • Image sharing for reference

Why: Reduces service mismatch issues.
